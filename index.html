About me

I’m an instructional designer and an educational technology graduate student at the University of Florida. Before focusing on education, I worked as a VFX artist in game development.

I still think about the visual feedback and interaction that make a game work. Now I’m interested in using those ideas to make learning tools clearer and more engaging.
